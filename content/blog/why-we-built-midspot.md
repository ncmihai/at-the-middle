Planning a trip with friends scattered across different cities is a special kind of chaos. Everyone opens their own browser tab, everyone checks different dates, and somehow nobody ever lands at the same time.

**midspot** started from that exact problem: a group of friends, each flying from home, wanting to meet somewhere in the middle — within a day of each other, and inside everyone's budget.

## How it works

Instead of everyone searching on their own, you tell midspot where each person is flying from, your dates (or a whole flexible month), how many nights, and a per-person budget. We then scan candidate destinations and rank them by two things:

1. **Arrival gap** — how close together everyone lands (the hard limit is 24 hours).
2. **Price** — the total outbound + return per person, within your budget.

You don't book through us — the booking happens on the partner's site (like Kiwi.com) through an affiliate link, at the same price for you.

## What's next

This blog is where we'll share what we learn along the way: where groups actually met, how the arrival-gap ranking behaves in practice, and the occasional note on flight-data APIs. It's also part of why the site exists — original content, written by us, about meeting in the middle.
